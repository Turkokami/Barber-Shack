About the Shack

We opened The Barber Shack with one idea: bring back the classic barbershop experience. That means real conversation, a hot towel, and a cut that looks as good on day fourteen as it does on day one.

Our barbers have a combined 40+ years behind the chair. Whether it's a skin fade, a beard sculpt, or a straight-razor shave, you're in steady hands.
